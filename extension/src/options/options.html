Groundtruth

Audits ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini answers in-place using the Groundtruth multi-agent verification API. Point the extension at a running Groundtruth backend below.

The extension calls {base}/api/audit and {base}/api/dehallucinate. Use http://localhost:3000 for local dev, or your deployed URL. Non-localhost URLs require granting host access (you'll be prompted on save).
Active on: chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com.
Click the toolbar icon on any of those to open the launcher and start auditing.
